# Client

The mod works on any server. Everything it adds starts from the [hub](hub.md) (++h++):

- [Packet viewer](packets.md): what the server and the client send each other.
- [HUD inspector](hud-inspector.md): how the client lays out the HUD's texts.
- [Locations and local sets](locations.md): plugin locations drawn in the world, and your own lists of points.
- [Selection](selection.md): a box with a gizmo, for the AI or your own commands.
- [Plugin messages and helpers](plugin-messages.md): a log of plugin channels, copying your position.
- [Keys and commands](keys-commands.md): every key and client command.

The AI chats are described in [AI](../ai/index.md).
