# Client

The mod works on any server. Everything it adds starts from the [hub](hub.md) (++h++):

- [Packet viewer](packets.md): what the server and the client send each other.
- [HUD inspector](hud-inspector.md): how the client lays out the HUD's texts.
- [Locations and local sets](locations.md): plugin locations drawn in the world, and your own lists of points.
- [Editor mode](editor.md) (++b++): the building tools below behind one key, with a panel of their keys.
- [Selection](selection.md): a box with a gizmo, for the AI or your own commands.
- [Asset library](library.md): the server's builds tagged as assets and its schematics, turned around and placed in
  the world (needs the Foreman plugin).
- [Model browser](models.md): every item model of the loaded resource packs in 3D, placed as a display or as
  furniture (placing needs the Foreman plugin).
- [Display editor](displays.md): select item, block and text displays (furniture too) by what they draw, and move,
  turn and scale them with a gizmo (saving needs the Foreman plugin).
- [Plugin messages and helpers](plugin-messages.md): a log of plugin channels, copying your position.
- [Resource packs](resource-packs.md): faster reloads that keep the game going, their timings and their warnings.
- [Keys and commands](keys-commands.md): every key and client command.

Foreman's building tools ([editor mode](editor.md)) save builds as assets and place them again, edit display entities
where they stand, and mark the areas, points and locations gameplay uses, to see the layout in the world and let the
server attach features to it.

The AI chats are described in [AI](../ai/index.md).
